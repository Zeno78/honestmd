FOR PATIENTS

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What is HonestMD?
A: HonestMD is a physician review platform where ONLY verified healthcare 
professionals can review doctors. Unlike typical review sites, our reviewers 
are surgical technologists, nurses, physician assistants, and fellow physicians 
who've actually worked alongside the doctors they review.

Q: How is this different from Healthgrades or Vitals?
A: Traditional sites allow anyone to review, leading to fake reviews and 
focus on parking/wait times. HonestMD:
- Verifies every reviewer's medical credentials
- Protects reviewer anonymity (no retaliation)
- Focuses on clinical competence, not amenities
- Reviews come from healthcare professionals, not patients

Q: Can I trust these reviews?
A: Yes. Every reviewer is verified through:
1. Medical license or hospital badge verification
2. Identity encrypted and protected
3. Reviews moderated for accuracy
4. Fraud detection systems
5. Physician dispute process for inaccuracies

Reviews are opinions based on professional observation, not patient outcomes 
or personal relationships.

Q: Why are reviewers anonymous?
A: Healthcare professionals face retaliation risks for honest feedback. 
Anonymity protects their jobs while ensuring honest assessment of clinical 
quality. We verify their credentials but protect their identities.

Q: How do I know the reviews are real?
A: We verify that:
- Reviewer has appropriate medical credentials
- Reviewer works (or worked) in relevant clinical setting
- Review meets our quality standards
- No patterns suggesting fraud or coordinated attacks

See our verification process: [link]

Q: Can physicians remove bad reviews?
A: No. Physicians can only:
- Dispute factually incorrect information
- Request removal of policy violations (profanity, PHI, defamation)
- Post a public response to provide context

We investigate all disputes and remove reviews only if inaccurate or 
policy-violating. See our Dispute Resolution Policy: [link]

Q: How many reviews should I consider credible?
A: We recommend:
- 1-2 reviews: Directional only
- 3-5 reviews: Patterns emerge
- 5+ reviews: High confidence in assessment

Look at the rating distribution (% of 5-star vs 1-star) and read 
individual reviews for context, not just the average.

Q: What if a physician has no reviews?
A: This means either:
- No verified reviewer has submitted a review yet
- Physician is new to practice
- Physician works in a setting with few eligible reviewers

No reviews ≠ bad physician. It just means no data yet.

Q: Can I review a physician as a patient?
A: Not currently. Phase 1 focuses on professional peer review only. 
We may add verified patient reviews in Phase 3 with strict verification 
to prevent fraud.

Q: Is HonestMD free to use?
A: Yes. Searching physicians and reading reviews is 100% free forever.

Q: How do you make money?
A: Physicians can pay for:
- Priority Listing ($149/month): Appear first in search results
- Priority Contact ($99/month): Direct scheduling link, updated info

We never remove negative reviews or favor paying physicians in ratings.

Q: What if I can't find my doctor?
A: We're in alpha testing. Coverage is limited. The database includes:
- All US physicians with NPI numbers (searchable)
- Reviews only for physicians who have been reviewed

If your doctor isn't reviewed yet, you can:
- Share HonestMD with healthcare workers you know
- Request review from your surgical team (if applicable)

FOR HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONALS (POTENTIAL REVIEWERS)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Who can become a verified reviewer?
A: Currently accepting:
- MDs and DOs (all specialties)
- Surgical technologists (CST, CSFA)
- Registered nurses (RN, OR nurses, floor nurses)
- Physician assistants (PA)
- Nurse practitioners (NP)
- CRNAs

Phase 2 will expand to CNAs, medical assistants, and other clinical roles 
with scope-appropriate review categories.

Q: How do I become a verified reviewer?
WORKFLOW:

Step 1: Apply
→ Email: honestmd.net@gmail.com
→ Subject: "Become a Verified Reviewer"
→ We send you application link

Step 2: Submit Credentials
→ Upload hospital badge OR medical license photo
→ Provide: Name, email, specialty, workplace (optional)
→ Can blur your photo on badge
→ Can cover employee ID number

Step 3: Verification (24-48 hours)
→ We verify credentials against state boards
→ Encrypt your real identity
→ Create anonymous reviewer account

Step 4: Start Reviewing
→ Log in with your anonymous username
→ Search for physicians you've worked with
→ Submit honest reviews

Timeline: 24-48 hours from application to verified reviewer

Q: How is my identity protected?
A: Multiple layers:
1. Real name encrypted (AES-256) - we can't even see it without decryption
2. Public reviews show only anonymous username
3. IP addresses not stored with reviews
4. No correlation between your identity and your reviews
5. Even if physicians dispute, we NEVER reveal reviewer identity

Q: What can I review physicians on?
A: 8 categories:
- Clinical Skill - Technical ability, procedure competence
- Bedside Manner - Patient interaction, empathy
- Communication - Clarity, listening, explanation
- Team Collaboration - Respect for staff, teamwork
- Decision Making - Clinical judgment, evidence-based
- Work Ethic - Preparedness, reliability, professionalism
- Teaching Ability - Mentorship, education (if applicable)
- Crisis Management - Performance under pressure

Plus overall rating (1-5 stars) and narrative comments.

Q: Can I review physicians I no longer work with?
A: Yes! You can review any physician you've worked alongside, even if:
- You've changed jobs
- Physician has retired
- You worked together years ago

Just ensure your review is based on actual professional observation.

Q: Will the physician know who I am?
A: Absolutely not. Even if they dispute the review, we conduct the entire 
investigation anonymously. They will NEVER know your identity.

Q: What if I make a mistake in my review?
A: You can:
- Edit your review anytime (must be logged in)
- Delete your review if you want to retract it
- Contact us to update information

Your anonymous identity is maintained throughout.

Q: Can I get in trouble at work for leaving a review?
A: No, because:
- Your identity is protected
- Reviews are anonymous
- We never disclose reviewer information
- Peer review is legally protected in most states

However, don't:
- Disclose patient information (HIPAA violation)
- Make false claims
- Violate our content policy

Q: How long does it take to write a review?
A: 5-10 minutes typically:
- Select physician
- Rate 8 categories (1-5 stars each)
- Write 100-500 word narrative
- Submit

Q: Can I review multiple physicians?
A: Yes! Review as many as you want. The more quality reviews, the 
better the platform.

Q: Do I get paid for reviews?
A: No. This is community-driven. We don't pay for reviews to avoid 
bias and fraud.

Q: What if I disagree with another review?
A: Different perspectives are expected. Reviews are subjective opinions 
based on professional observation. You can:
- Submit your own review with different perspective
- Vote reviews as "helpful" or not (coming in Phase 2)

Don't create multiple accounts to dispute other reviews - that's fraud.

FOR PHYSICIANS BEING REVIEWED

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: I found myself on HonestMD. How did this happen?
A: Any US physician with an NPI number can be searched on our platform 
(we use the public NPI Registry). However, you only appear in results 
if someone has reviewed you OR a user specifically searches for you.

Q: Can I remove my profile?
A: No. NPI data is public information. However:
- Having a profile doesn't mean you have reviews
- If you have no reviews, your profile is minimal (just NPI data)
- If you have reviews, they're protected by peer review privilege

Q: I don't like a review. What can I do?
WORKFLOW:

Option 1: Dispute the Review (if factually wrong or policy violation)

Step 1: Identify the issue
→ Factually incorrect information?
→ Violates content policy (profanity, defamation, PHI)?
→ Suspected fraud?

Step 2: Submit dispute
→ Email: disputes@honestmd.net
→ Include: Your NPI, link to review, reason, evidence
→ We respond within 24 hours

Step 3: Investigation (3-7 days)
→ We review the claim
→ Contact original reviewer anonymously
→ Make determination

Step 4: Resolution
→ Review removed (if inaccurate/violates policy)
→ Review edited (if minor violation)
→ Review remains + you can post response
→ Detailed explanation provided

Option 2: Post a Public Response (if review is fair but you want to provide context)

Step 1: Verify your identity
→ Email: physician-response@honestmd.net
→ We verify via NPI + medical license

Step 2: Draft response
→ 500 words maximum
→ Professional tone (no personal attacks)
→ Provide context or explanation
→ Example: "I appreciate the feedback. Regarding the communication 
   concern, I've since implemented a new post-op follow-up system."

Step 3: Moderation
→ We review response (no attacks, no policy violations)
→ Approve within 24 hours

Step 4: Published
→ Response appears below review
→ Clearly labeled "Physician Response"

Option 3: Private Resolution (rare)

→ We can facilitate anonymous conversation with reviewer
→ Both parties must consent
→ Mediated resolution possible
→ If resolved, both can note resolution

See full Dispute Resolution Policy: [link]

Q: Can I pay to remove bad reviews?
A: Absolutely not. We never remove reviews for money. This would 
destroy platform credibility.

You can only:
- Dispute inaccurate/policy-violating reviews (free)
- Post public response (free)
- Pay for Priority Listing (but this doesn't affect ratings or remove reviews)

Q: Will this hurt my practice?
A: Depends on the reviews. If you receive consistently high ratings, 
this will HELP your practice by:
- Building patient trust
- Differentiating you from competitors
- Providing social proof

If you receive negative reviews:
- Use them as constructive feedback
- Respond professionally to provide context
- Improve based on peer input

Most physicians receive mixed reviews (4+ overall). Perfect 5.0 is rare 
and sometimes less credible.

Q: Why should I trust anonymous reviewers?
A: Because anonymity enables honesty. Named reviews risk:
- Retaliation
- Career damage
- Professional relationship strain

Anonymous reviews from verified healthcare professionals are more honest 
than public reviews from anyone on the internet.

Q: Can I claim my profile?
A: Yes! Coming in Phase 2 (Months 4-6). Benefits:
- Update practice information
- Add profile photo
- Respond to reviews easily
- View performance insights
- See analytics (profile views, clicks)

Email physician-verify@honestmd.net to get notified when available.

Q: What if someone is targeting me with fake reviews?
A: We have fraud detection for:
- Coordinated negative campaigns
- Multiple reviews from same IP
- Sudden influx of negative reviews
- Pattern analysis

If detected:
- We pause publication
- Investigate thoroughly
- Contact reviewers for additional verification
- Remove confirmed fake reviews
- Ban fraudulent accounts
- Potentially involve law enforcement

Report suspected fraud: fraud@honestmd.net

Q: Can I see who reviewed me?
A: No. Reviewer identities are permanently encrypted and protected. 
This is core to our model.

Q: What if the review is opinion, not fact?
A: Opinion-based reviews are allowed if:
- Based on professional observation
- Not defamatory
- Relevant to clinical competence

Example:
✓ "Dr. Smith is competent but not exceptional" - Opinion, allowed
✗ "Dr. Smith is incompetent and dangerous" - May be defamatory if not substantiated

If you disagree with an opinion, post a response providing your perspective.

Q: I'm a great physician. How do I get positive reviews?
A: We don't solicit reviews for you. However:
- Continue providing excellent care
- Treat team members with respect
- Communicate effectively
- Over time, reviews will reflect this

Don't:
- Ask staff to review you (creates bias)
- Offer incentives for reviews (violates policy)
- Create fake accounts (permanent ban)

Q: How does your verification work?
A: Multi-step process:

1. Credential Collection
→ Reviewer uploads hospital badge OR medical license
→ Name, credential, workplace visible
→ Can blur photo, cover employee ID

2. Verification
→ Cross-check against state medical boards (MDs/DOs)
→ Verify hospital employment (if badge provided)

3. Identity Encryption

→ Anonymous username assigned

4. Approval
→ Reviewer notified (24-48 hours)
→ Can start reviewing immediately

5. Ongoing Monitoring
→ Annual re-verification
→ Pattern analysis for fraud
→ Reviewer quality scoring

POLICIES & LEGAL

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Are reviews protected by law?
A: In most states, peer review is protected by peer review privilege laws. 
This provides qualified immunity for honest professional assessment.

However:
- Reviews must be opinion-based or factual
- No defamation (false factual claims)
- No malicious intent
- Based on professional observation

We consult legal counsel on disputes.

Q: What if I get sued?
A: If you're a verified reviewer and followed our guidelines:
- Your identity is protected (anonymous)
- Reviews covered by peer review privilege in most states
- We provide legal support if needed
- Platform bears legal responsibility

Don't worry - anonymous peer review is legally established in healthcare.

Q: Do you comply with HIPAA?
A: Yes. Reviewers must:
- Never include patient names or identifying details
- Generalize case descriptions
- Focus on physician behavior, not patient outcomes

We automatically flag and remove PHI (protected health information).

Q: What about GDPR and CCPA?
A: We comply with:
- GDPR (EU data protection)
- CCPA (California privacy)
- Right to access data
- Right to deletion
- Opt-out of data sale (we don't sell data anyway)

Contact: privacy@honestmd.net
